# CubeSat Mission Business Analytics: Launch Cost, Mission Feasibility & ROI Analytics

## Project overview
An Excel-based business analytics project comparing 40 hypothetical CubeSat mission scenarios across 1U, 3U, 6U and 12U classes. The model estimates development, launch, integration, testing and operating costs; lifetime revenue; net profit; ROI; break-even period; risk, demand and payload-fit scores; and a feasibility decision.

## Files
- `CubeSat_Mission_Business_Analytics.xlsx` — dashboard, scenario data, assumptions, methodology and submission guide.
- `cubesat_scenario_data.csv` — flat dataset for re-use in Excel, SQL or Power BI.

## Key metrics
- **Total Cost** = Development + Launch + Integration + Testing + (Annual Operating Cost × Lifetime)
- **Total Revenue** = Annual Revenue × Lifetime
- **Net Profit** = Total Revenue − Total Cost
- **ROI (%)** = (Net Profit / Total Cost) × 100
- **Break-even (years)** = Total Cost / Annual Revenue

## Important data note
This is an educational scenario model. Costs, revenues, lifetimes and scores are illustrative assumptions, not verified current market quotes or audited mission outcomes. Replace them with documented, cited sources before presenting them as real-world market data. Feasibility scores and rules are heuristics, not engineering certification or financial advice.

## How to use
1. Open the Excel workbook and start at `Dashboard`.
2. Filter `Scenario Data` by CubeSat class, mission type or decision.
3. Review `Assumptions` and `Methodology` before interpreting results.
4. Add a `Sources` sheet if you research and replace assumptions with sourced data.
