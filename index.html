Methodology

Total cost

Development + launch + integration + testing + annual operating cost × lifetime.

Total revenue

Annual revenue × assumed mission lifetime.

ROI

(Net profit ÷ total cost) × 100.

Break-even

Total cost ÷ annual revenue, assuming revenue begins immediately and stays constant.

Data integrity: costs, revenues, mission lifetimes and scores are illustrative assumptions created for this student project; they are not verified market quotations or audited mission results. Feasibility and risk scores are heuristic indicators.
